STEP 05 · KNOW YOUR KHATA

Khata types & services in Bengaluru

Khata terminology can be confusing. Here is a simple explanation to help you understand the common terms.

A-KHATA

A-Khata

A category of municipal property record associated with properties meeting applicable requirements.

FORM-B

B-Khata / Form-B

Certain properties are recorded under a separate Form-B category. The record itself should not be treated as a complete statement of title or building legality.

SERVICE

New Khata

A service/process for creating a Khata record for an eligible property.

SERVICE

Khata Transfer

Transfer of an existing Khata record to the new owner, subject to applicable requirements.

DIGITAL

e-Khata

Digital property/Khata record and related online process for eligible properties.

APPLICABLE CASES

B-to-A / Single Plot

An applicable route for eligible cases. Actual eligibility should be checked against current rules and property records.

A-Khata vs B-Khata — important clarification

Khata classification should not be treated as a simple “legal vs illegal property” test. Title, building approval, land-use, tax and other statutory matters can involve separate requirements.
